# The AI never sees People

The AI planner may read a Project's description, a Leader's notes and non-personal church data (facilities, calendar, the structure of past Projects), but never the directory, names or care details. It plans the work as open slots ("2 drivers"), and humans choose who fills them. This keeps congregant data out of a third-party model and keeps people decisions with people, at the cost of an AI that can't suggest volunteers by name.
